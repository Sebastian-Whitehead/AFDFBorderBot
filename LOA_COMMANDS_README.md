>>> # LOA (Leave of Absence) Commands Guide

` /loa <end_date> [reason] ` — Submit or update your LOA (e.g. ` /loa 2025-08-25 Vacation `)
` /loa-list ` — See all active LOAs
` /loa-cancel ` — Cancel your LOA

- Use these in the LOA channel only.
- Only one active LOA per user.
- For help, contact @parz1val.
